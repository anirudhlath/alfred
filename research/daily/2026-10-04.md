## Memory-decay eval with bge-m3 on the GPU (EXP-008)

Same 60-day run as EXP-007 (seed 7, Redis 8.2.10), embeddings from `BAAI/bge-m3`
served by the shared vLLM instance on the RTX 4090 (`EMBEDDING_BACKEND=openai`, one
input per request). Run `2026-10-04T062412`; rows in `data/memory-decay.csv`;
analysis in `experiments/EXP-008-memory-decay-bge-m3.md`. An earlier run,
`2026-10-04T060430`, predates the concurrency fix and matched it on every recall
number; its rows are in the CSV too.

**Events processed per policy:** 13,316 memories, 72 retrievals, 1,440 decay passes,
33 probes, 26,632 write-path embeddings — about 90,000 embedding requests to vLLM
per run of three policies.

**Decay pass latency (ms):**

| policy | p50 | p95 | p99 | max | embeds | migrated |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| no_decay | 0 | 0 | 0 | 0 | 0 | 0 |
| branch | 269 | 341 | 370 | 422 | 5,440 | 9,585 |
| oracle | 180 | 256 | 284 | 319 | 5,440 | 9,585 |

Wall time per policy 57 / 386 / 276 s (EmbeddingGemma on CPU: 175 / 653 / 519 s).

**Token usage:** none.

**Results:** involuntary hit@10 at day 60 91% without decay and 76% with it
(EmbeddingGemma: 76% / 67%); `memory_recall_memories` 30/33 under every policy
(28/33); no search misses; 2.9–3.4 non-targets in the top 10 (1.8–2.0).

**Anomalies:**
- A backlog pass (59 days without decay, then the branch's pass) moved only 1,890 of
  9,357 memories on its first pass: every migration started at once and exhausted
  redis-py's 100-connection pool ("Too many connections", 14,546 warnings in a
  re-run). Fixed on the branch: at most 8 migrations at a time, and compression moves
  originals before writing a summary of those that moved. After the fix the pass moves
  all 9,357 in 16.6 s (CPU, re-measured: 145 s), with no warnings.
